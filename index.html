Welcome to our dojo

An old tradition. A continuing practice.

Toowoomba Jujitsu Kai is a traditional jujitsu dojo teaching Danzan Ryu Jujitsu and Seifukujitsu. Our training draws on a martial tradition that values both effective technique and the knowledge to help others.

Jujitsu develops more than physical ability. Through repeated practice with training partners, students work on coordination, agility, awareness, self-defence and personal discipline. Progress comes through patience, effort and a willingness to keep learning.

Whether you're curious about traditional martial arts or looking for a new challenge, you're welcome to get in touch and arrange an introductory session.

What we practise

More than technique. A way of training.

Our practice develops physical ability alongside the qualities that make training worthwhile: character, empathy, self-control and respect for others.

01

Character

Build discipline, perseverance and self-control through consistent practice. The way we train matters as much as the techniques we learn.

02

Self-defence

Study traditional jujitsu techniques with an emphasis on practical application, body mechanics, awareness and responding appropriately to challenges.

03

Empathy

Training depends on trust and cooperation. Learn with your partners, look out for their well-being and develop an appreciation for the responsibility that comes with martial skill.

Danzan Ryu Jujitsu · Seifukujitsu

Learn the art. Practise the details.

Jujitsu is learned through doing. Training combines the study of traditional techniques with the physical coordination, timing and control needed to practise them with a partner.

Our dojo also teaches Seifukujitsu, the healing arts associated with our tradition. Together, these practices reflect the idea of the warrior-as-healer: developing skill while understanding the importance of care and responsibility.

  • Study Danzan Ryu Jujitsu and traditional techniques.
  • Develop practical self-defence skills and awareness.
  • Improve coordination, agility and physical fitness.
  • Explore Seifukujitsu and the healing side of the tradition.
  • Build character and learn through cooperative practice.

No previous martial arts experience is needed to enquire about an introductory session. Contact us and we'll discuss the best way for you to experience the training.

Danzan Ryu is a traditional school of jujitsu that includes throws, joint techniques, grappling and other martial skills. Our training also encompasses Seifukujitsu, the healing arts associated with the tradition. Contact us to learn more about what we practise.
