Welcome to our dojo

Learn with purpose. Grow with every class.

Jujitsu is a martial art that rewards patience, awareness and consistent practice. It offers opportunities to develop physical skills while learning the importance of respect, self-control and perseverance.

At Toowoomba Jujitsu Kai, we want our website to be the beginning of your journey: a place to learn about our dojo, discover our approach and find out how to get involved.

The foundations

Stronger in skill. Stronger in character.

Martial arts is about more than learning techniques. The lessons developed through practice can extend well beyond the dojo.

01

Discipline

Build good habits through regular practice, patience and a commitment to continual learning.

02

Confidence

03

Respect

Value your training partners, listen carefully and contribute to a positive training environment.

Your martial arts journey

Every expert starts somewhere.

Taking the first step can feel daunting. The best way to discover whether jujitsu is right for you is to learn about the training available and speak with the dojo.

  • Learn at a pace appropriate to your experience.
  • Develop coordination and physical skills.
  • Practise technique and mindful movement.
  • Build knowledge through consistent training.
